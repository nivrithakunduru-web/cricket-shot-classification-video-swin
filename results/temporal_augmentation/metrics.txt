Experiment

Experiment: Temporal Augmentation

Pretrained Weights: Kinetics-400
Number of Classes: 10
Frames per Video: 32
Input Resolution: 224 x 224

Temporal Augmentation
---------------------
Training Sampling:
The video timeline is divided into 32 temporal segments.
One frame is randomly sampled from each segment.

The selected frames remain in chronological order.

Validation / Test Sampling:
Deterministic uniform sampling using 32 frame positions.

Short Video Handling:
Videos that are too short for random segment sampling use
deterministic uniform sampling.

Custom Spatial Augmentation:
None
